FOUNDER & DIRECTOR PROFILE

ATTIKUS EBENHACK

Combat Camera Veteran • Screenwriter • Director • Fine Art Photographer

Born and raised in Haiti, Attikus survived the catastrophic 2010 Haiti earthquake before moving to the United States with his adopted family as the eldest of eight blended children. His passion for storytelling was ignited as a child watching The Lord of the Ringsâ€”experiencing the transporting magic that only true cinema can wield.

He began in theater and acting, soaking in performance dynamics before serving six years in the U.S. Army as a Combat Camera Production Specialist. Operating cameras under high-stress, unpredictable, and dangerous conditions forged an unshakable technical mastery and deep empathy for human resilience.

"One of my main missions in life is to be a voice for the voiceless and shine art into areas that others overlook. There is magic when you see people who look like you on screen. There is power in creating a good plan, and being diligent to it." — Attikus Ebenhack, Feature Interview with CanvasRebel Magazine

Today, through Citadel Productions LLC and Photo By Attikus in Nashville, TN, he directs feature films, commercial campaigns for high-growth brands, and high-fashion editorial portraits.
